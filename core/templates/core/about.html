About RoadsideAuto

RoadsideAuto started as a mobile mechanic service, bringing servicing and repairs directly to customers across Melbourne rather than requiring a trip to a workshop. That same approach to honest, straightforward workmanship now continues from our workshop.

We explain the work before we start it, provide a clear quote, and only proceed once you've approved it — no surprise costs, no unnecessary upselling.

Alongside general mechanical servicing and repairs, we have a particular focus on 4x4 and touring vehicles — from routine maintenance and pre-trip inspections through to lift kits, bull bars, snorkels, and roof rack fitting.

We work on all makes and models, with particular experience in vehicles used for towing, touring, and off-road driving.
